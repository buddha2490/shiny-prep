## Bio

Hey everyone! I'm Brian, and I'm thrilled to be joining the Regeneron team remotely from Atlanta. I've spent the last 20 years in statistical computing and data science, starting as an R programmer back in 2007 and picking up Shiny in 2017. I've authored 9 R packages, spent 12 years in cancer research with 60+ publications, and led investigations with institutional partners in the NCI Cohort Consortium. For the last five years I directed a multidisciplinary data science consultancy that covered everything from Shiny development and bioinformatics in pharma to Posit DevOps and engineering.

More recently, as the services market shifted toward AI, I was focused on building discrete AI solutions, including automated SAS-to-R migrations, full-stack cloud governance platforms, agentic services, and all kind of random chatbots. Right now I'm most excited about R and Shiny development and finding ways to build AI solutions tailored to my clients problems. Outside of work, you'll find me doing astrophotography, tinkering with local LLMs, or hanging out with my wife and two kids.
